Purpose and limits
Operational readiness
This app does not predict debris flows, issue evacuation advice, or replace official watches, warnings, and instructions from public-safety agencies.

The readiness signal is a spatial intersection between a published USGS assessment footprint and the selected NOAA precipitation product. Days 1–3 use WPC Excessive Rainfall Outlooks; Days 3–7 and 8–14 use broader NOAA/WPC and NOAA/CPC precipitation-hazard outlooks. This indicates where closer monitoring may be warranted; it is not a debris-flow prediction.
